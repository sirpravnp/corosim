      Conduction

      What the patient brought with them. Fibrillation takes away the P waves, so the AV node shows only as a slowing of the irregular response. A left bundle branch block pushes every ST segment away from its QRS before any lesion does, so read it for change: concordant ST, or discordant elevation out of proportion to the S wave.

      Time scale

      Ischemia develops over about 20 s after occlusion and resolves with a time constant of about 8 s after reperfusion. Rate and AV conduction follow the territory; lost contraction and slow rates lower blood pressure, and lower pressure perfuses every coronary bed less.

      Treat

      Orders take clinical time to reach the artery (about 2 minutes pass per second at 1×), and muscle that stays ischemic dies as it waits. PCI stents the lesion; a fibrinolytic opens an occlusion in about half of patients and leaves a stenosis; a bypass feeds the vessel beyond the lesion and leaves the lesion itself. Support below changes what the patient can tolerate; it does not open the artery.

      Volume

      Blood loss and an IV bolus ramp in over about 10 s; ongoing hemorrhage keeps taking blood at the rate you set. What follows is the patient's own response: watch the rate, the pressure and the pulse pressure. A bolus of crystalloid mostly leaves the vessels; colloid, albumin and blood stay longer. Volume helps a right ventricular infarct and congests a failing left ventricle.

      Drugs

      Each choice has a cost: pressors add afterload, inotropes add demand and ectopy, nitrates drop the preload a right ventricle depends on, a beta blocker slows the heart and weakens contraction. Doses are fractions of the full effect.

      More drugs

      Receptor-level drugs of the core engine: epinephrine (beta and alpha), milrinone (inodilator), vasopressin (non-adrenergic pressor), nitroprusside (arterial and venous dilator), furosemide (diuretic). Doses are fractions of the full effect.

      Devices

      Settings appear when a device is on. Pacing captures the ventricle at the set rate. The balloon pump augments diastolic pressure and unloads the ventricle; an Impella pumps blood from the ventricle to the aorta (a wrong position cuts its flow); VA ECMO drains the right atrium and returns blood to the aorta.

      Ventilation

      Positive-pressure breaths raise intrathoracic pressure: they cut venous return and right ventricular filling, and unload a failing left ventricle. PEEP and tidal volume set how much.

      Assessment

      Issues are what the monitor shows now; warnings are choices that are doing harm. The guidelines list the next steps in order. Reset treatment takes support, orders and the procedure away and lets the heart start over.
