## Stochastic outcomes

Wilson 95% interval. Compared with a `p<lo>..<hi>` cell when one has been written.

| outcome | k/n | rate | 95% interval | spec |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fibrinolysis success | 14/24 | 58% | 39%-76% | no cell |
| Defibrillation of VF at 200 J, ROSC by 20 s after the shock | 22/24 | 92% | 74%-98% | no cell |
| VT or VF by 6 h after proximal LAD occlusion | 11/24 | 46% | 28%-65% | no cell |
| At least one PVC after reperfusion | 6/24 | 25% | 12%-45% | no cell |

- **Fibrinolysis success.** The plan's informal figure is about 55%. A 24-seed interval around 55% is about 35-74%, so a spec cell has to be that wide. No expectation cell is written here.
- **Defibrillation of VF at 200 J, ROSC by 20 s after the shock.** ROSC is a perfusing rhythm with output held for 10 s (roscSustainS), so it cannot show at the 25 s checkpoint, 5 s after the shock. Counted at 40 s. Rhythm can already be sinus while arrest status is still arrest. There is no expectation cell.
- **VT or VF by 6 h after proximal LAD occlusion.** Any episode of NSVT, monomorphic VT, polymorphic VT or VF. There is no expectation cell.
- **At least one PVC after reperfusion.** Share of seeds with pvcsPer10s > 0 at 40 s (after PCI at 10 s). There is no expectation cell.
