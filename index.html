Time scale

Ischemia develops over about 20 s after occlusion and resolves with a time constant of about 8 s after reperfusion. Rate and AV conduction follow the territory: anterior ischemia speeds the heart; inferior ischemia, and loss of the SA- or AV-node artery, slow it and block conduction. Lost contraction and slow rates lower blood pressure, and lower pressure then perfuses every coronary bed less.
